Stay Safe Online Checklist:

1.Use strong passwords: Create unique passwords for each account and avoid using the same password 
everywhere.

2.Enable two-factor authentication : Add an extra layer of security to important accounts like email and banking.

3.Do not share OTPs or passwords: Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

4.Verify links before clicking: Always check the sender’s email address or website URL before opening links.

5.Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks: Do not access banking or personal accounts on unsecured networks.

6.Limit sharing personal information: Avoid posting sensitive details like phone number, address, or ID proofs online.

7.Be cautious of urgent offers: Messages claiming “limited time offers” or “urgent action required” are often scams.
UPI Safety Tip 1: Never approve “collect request” payments unless you initiated the transaction.
UPI Safety Tip 2: Always double-check the UPI ID or QR code before sending money

8.Report Cybercrime
Report online fraud at: cybercrime.gov.in
Helpline Number: 1930 (24×7)
