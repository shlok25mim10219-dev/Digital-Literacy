## Project Overview
This Digital Literacy Project was completed as part of the CSE0001 course at VIT Bhopal. The objective of this project was to develop a practical understanding of digital tools, online presence, communication, and cyber safety.
As a Student Digital Ambassador, I created a portfolio consisting of five tasks that demonstrate my ability to use digital platforms responsibly and effectively. These tasks include creating an infographic, building a professional digital profile, exploring coding and collaboration tools, practicing email etiquette, and studying cybercrime awareness.
This project helped me understand that digital literacy is not just about using technology, but also about making responsible and informed decisions in digital environments.

##Module Summaries
###  Module 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness
Created an infographic using Canva covering key topics such as digital literacy, safe internet practices, and professional communication. This helped in understanding how to present information visually.
###  Module 2 – Digital Portfolio
Built profiles on LinkedIn, GitHub, and Kaggle to establish a professional online presence. Learned the importance of maintaining an authentic digital identity.
###  Module 3 – Platforms & Collaboration
Solved a coding challenge on HackerRank and created a Google Form quiz. This improved problem-solving skills and introduced collaborative tools for data collection.
###  Module 4 – Email Etiquette & Communication
Wrote professional emails and created a social media checklist. Learned the importance of clear, respectful, and structured digital communication.
###  Module 5 – Cybercrime Awareness
Studied identity theft and created a prevention checklist. Gained awareness about online risks and safe digital practices.
