### &nbsp;     Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist|                   **Do’s**|                   **Don’ts**||-|-||Maintain professionalism: Share content that reflects your values, skills, and interests positively.<br />|Do not share confidential information: Avoid posting personal data like phone numbers, passwords, or financial details.<br /><br /><br />||Think before posting: Always consider the long-term impact of what you share online.<br />|Do not spread misinformation: Never share unverified or false content.||Protect your privacy: Use strong privacy settings and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.<br />|Avoid inappropriate language or content: Offensive posts can harm your reputation.||Verify information: Check the authenticity of news or content before sharing it with others.<br />|Do not overshare: Posting too much personal information can increase security risks.||Engage respectfully: Communicate politely and maintain a respectful tone in comments and messages.<br />|Avoid impulsive posting: Do not post or comment in anger or without thinking.|
